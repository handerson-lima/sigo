## A — Sóbria

Use case: ui-mockup. Create one high-fidelity raster design concept for SIGO construction-management software, desktop screen "Construtoras". Landscape 16:10-ish, sharp professional interface, flat front-facing screen with no physical monitor, no perspective. Portuguese UI. Same structure for comparison: left sidebar with gold geometric S-inspired SIGO brand mark and SIGO wordmark, items "Construtoras" selected and "Minha conta"; top bar with breadcrumb "SIGO / Construtoras" and small avatar "CM". Main area headline "Suas construtoras", subtitle "Escolha uma empresa para continuar.", search field "Buscar construtora". Three generous company cards in one row, fictional companies "Horizonte Engenharia", "Vértice Construções", "Base Construtora". Cards include tasteful fictional company logo, readable company name, secondary "Construtora", and "Acessar construtora" affordance; third has elegant BC initials fallback rather than a logo. Preserve logo proportions within dedicated neutral logo area. No fake metrics, charts, revenue, creation/upload buttons or unrelated features. Display title of concept at bottom outside app frame. This is a visual exploration, not implementation. Palette based on SIGO blue #2196F3, accessible action blue #1565C0, gold #FCA906, ink #0F172A, light background #F8FAFC. Prioritize exceptional typography, balanced proportions and polished company cards. Direction A: "A — Sóbria e sofisticada". Restrained premium corporate product design. Mostly off-white and white, thin neutral borders, subtle diffuse shadows, generous whitespace, navy text. Slim dark navy sidebar. Blue only for active indicator and restrained action links. Gold extremely sparing at SIGO mark and tiny details. Cards feel refined and confident with clean spacious logo presentation; no gradients, no saturated large blocks. Visually sophisticated through precise alignment, hierarchy and breathing room.
